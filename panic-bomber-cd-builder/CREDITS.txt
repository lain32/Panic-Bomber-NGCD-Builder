Panic Bomber Neo Geo CD builder

Game content comes from the user's supplied Panic Bomber cartridge ROMs
and existing CD port. Original game and music credits remain with their
respective creators and rightsholders. This builder does not claim authorship
or grant rights to the underlying game or included audio.

The conversion incorporates the Test 3 sample-bank handshake and Test 4
ending-audio correction developed in this project, and preserves the source
CD port's in-disc metadata and notices.

Finalized audio and emulation checks used NeoCD/libretro, source revision:
3118c6901787e863e80e79170d02d47657b3b0ab
https://github.com/libretro/neocd_libretro
No emulator binary, emulator source, BIOS or cartridge ROM ZIP is included.
